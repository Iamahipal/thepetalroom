Our story

A little room full of petals & patience

The Petal Room began with a hook, a basket of yarn, and a simple wish — to give people flowers that never have to be thrown away. Every piece is crocheted slowly by hand, stitch after stitch, until an ordinary strand of yarn blossoms into something you'll want to keep forever.

From tiny keychains to full bouquets, each order is made just for you — with premium finishing, thoughtful detail, and a lot of love.
